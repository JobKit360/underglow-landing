Privacy

Draft for the early beta. Last updated 27 November 2026.

The short version: your projects stay on your computer. This website keeps anonymous visit counts and, if you join the waitlist, your email. We keep your account details so you can sign in, and the app talks to a few outside services to work. This is a working draft that has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

What stays on your computer

Your project files, agent chats and settings are stored on the computer that runs UnderGlow. We do not upload your project folders to our servers.

What we keep about you

  • Account: your email address and a securely stored (hashed) password, kept by our sign-in provider.
  • Plan status: which plan or access your account has, so the app knows what to unlock.
  • Payments: if paid plans are offered, payment is handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Emails: we send sign-in and account emails (such as confirming your address or resetting a password) through an email provider.

Services the app and this website talk to

ServiceWhy
Sign-in providerSign-in and account records, from the app
Anthropic (Claude)Your agents run through Claude Code on your own Claude account. What an agent reads from your project to do its job is sent to that service under their terms and privacy policy, not ours
StripePayments, if you buy a plan

This website

We keep simple, anonymous visit counts so we can tell which version of the page works best. For each visit we record:

  • that the page was viewed, and whether the screen was phone-sized
  • which headline version you were shown
  • where you came from: the referring site and any campaign tag in the link (such as utm_source)

This is not linked to your name or email. Your browser remembers which headline you saw (in its local storage) so it stays the same; we set no cookies.

If you join the waitlist, we also store your email address and any optional answers you give (such as what you would pay), together with the headline and source above. We use it only to tell you about UnderGlow and to understand demand. The data is stored with our database provider, Supabase.

No advertising or ad trackers, and we never sell your data. Our host may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and page requested) for security and reliability. To be removed from the waitlist or have your data deleted, email us (below).

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the account data we hold, and to close your account.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time. Your project files are never removed by doing so.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above.
