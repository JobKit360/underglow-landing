3. Give it a job

Describe what you want in plain words. Watch it work, or check back later.

Questions

Straight answers

Do I need to know how to code?

  • No. You describe jobs in plain words. That is the whole point.

Which AI does it use?

  • It uses the AI account you already have. You stay in control of that account.

Will I get surprise bills?

  • No. We plan one flat monthly price with no credit system, and you will see exactly what is included before you pay anything.

Is it ready?

  • Not yet. We are building it now. Waitlist members get in first, in the order they joined.

Is my stuff private?

  • UnderGlow runs on a computer you own. We do not host your files.

  • Not by default. Helpers draft and prepare, and you approve before anything is sent or paid. AI can make mistakes, so you stay in charge.
