Why UnderGlow

Running agents is free. Babysitting them is not.

You can run several agents today with tmux, a VPN and remote desktop. Then it is your job to wire it up and keep it alive.

Doing it yourself

  • Set up a VPN, port forwarding or remote desktop
  • Keep terminals and sessions alive, hunt for who is stuck
  • Agents trip over each other's files and ports
  • Tied to your desk to approve anything

With UnderGlow (what we are building)

  • Install on your host, sign in on your laptop and phone, done
  • One screen shows every agent and what it is doing
  • A code map shows what each one changed
  • Your phone gets the question, you tap approve
