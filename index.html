Approve from your phone

Your agent hits a question or a change that needs your yes. You get it on your phone, tap approve, and it carries on. Nothing to install: it opens in your phone browser.

  • Answer agents from anywhere

Run a whole team of agents

Give each agent a job and watch them all at once, on one screen. Claude Code, Codex and others side by side. Step in, redirect or stop any of them in a click.

  • One chat per agent, all visible
  • See each step as it happens
  • Stop or steer any agent

Gates exactly where you want them

Lay out your own steps, from your agents to a test version to your live site, and put a gate on any step that waits for your yes. Or use none. It is your workflow.

  • Add or remove gates any time

  • Click a step to see what it does

See what every agent changed
