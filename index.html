Questions

Straight answers

Do I need to know how to code?

  • No. You describe jobs in plain words. That is the whole point.

Do I need my own AI plan?

  • Yes: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or similar. Helpers use the official AI tools on your computer, signed in to your own account. We never charge for AI use, so there are no surprise credits.

Is it ready?

  • Not yet. We are building it now. Waitlist members get in first, in the order they joined.

Is my stuff private?

  • UnderGlow runs on a computer you own. We do not host your files.

Can it send or buy things without me?

  • Not by default. Helpers draft and prepare, and you approve before anything is sent or paid. AI can make mistakes, so you stay in charge.
