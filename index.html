What it does

Everything you need, in one friendly window

Hand a job to your AI helpers and watch it get done. No more switching between a dozen windows, tabs and notes.

Tell a helper what you want

Write it the way you'd say it to a person. Your helper gets to work in your project, and you can follow along, stop it or change direction at any time.

  • One chat for each helper
  • See every step it takes
  • Stop or steer it any time

Watch the work move along

A simple picture shows a change travelling from your helpers to a test version and then to your live site. It stops at a gate, and you decide when it goes through.

  • Plain-language steps
  • Say yes or hold back with one tap
  • Click a step to see what it does

See your project at a glance

Your files appear as a glowing web of dots. Watch a helper read (blue) and change (yellow) things as it moves around.

  • See how things connect
  • Spot where work is happening
  • Hover any dot to see its file

A safe room to try things out

An optional extra: each helper gets its own separate test computer inside yours, so it can try things out without touching the rest of your PC.

  • Off until you turn it on
  • Watch its screen inside UnderGlow
  • Nothing leaks into your own files

A to-do board your helpers keep up to date

A simple board with To do, In progress, Waiting test and Complete. Helpers add tasks and move them along as they work, so you always know what's next.

  • Cards follow the helper doing them
  • Click or drag to move things yourself
  • Always see what is next

Check in from your phone

Open UnderGlow from your phone's web browser, at home on your Wi-Fi or privately from anywhere. A little badge tells you the connection is safe.

  • Same account, same app
  • Your files stay on your computer
  • Safe-connection badge
